# Sources

Research starts here. An idea agrees with these experts **and** with
Gustavo's mottos. Where they disagree, his motto wins and the idea says so
on camera: "GQ dice X; yo prefiero Y, porque…". That makes good content.

Search them with WebSearch (`site:gq.com …`, `site:gq.com.mx …`) and read with
WebFetch. Note the URL and date of every claim an idea rests on.

## Style: classic (the foundation)

| Source | Use it for |
|---|---|
| GQ (gq.com) and GQ México y Latinoamérica (gq.com.mx) | What is current each season; his favourite. |
| Put This On (putthison.com) | Classic menswear explained simply; where he learned. |
| Permanent Style (permanentstyle.com) | Quality, fabrics, fit, how a garment ages. |

| Mr Porter, The Journal | Wardrobe staples, how to wear one piece many ways. |
| The Rake | Classic tailoring and the old icons. |

Icons for the tone: Steve McQueen, Paul Newman, Cary Grant, Gianni Agnelli,
Marcello Mastroianni.

## Fitness ("treat yourself as an athlete")

Evidence-based coaches only. No supplement hype, no miracle routines. Health
claims need a study or a clear expert consensus, and medical topics end with
"consulta a tu médico".

| Source | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Jeff Nippard | Training explained with research. |
| Andy Galpin | Performance, the athlete mindset, recovery. |
| Peter Attia, *Outlive* | Long-term health, strength and cardio as you age. |
| Stronger By Science (Greg Nuckols) | Strength and hypertrophy research. |
| Renaissance Periodization (Mike Israetel) | Hypertrophy, diet. |
| Powerexplosive (David Marchante), Sergio Peinado | The Spanish-language fitness market. |

## Charisma (extreme ownership, humble confidence)

| Source | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Marcus Aurelius, *Meditaciones* | Focus on what you control, ignore the insidious; his anchor. Seneca and Epictetus for support. |
| Jocko Willink and Leif Babin, *Extreme Ownership* | Ownership at work and at home. |
| Ryan Holiday: *The Obstacle Is the Way*, *Ego Is the Enemy*, Daily Stoic | Stoicism made practical; humility with ambition. |
| Olivia Fox Cabane, *The Charisma Myth* | Charisma as presence, power and warmth; learnable. |
| Dale Carnegie, *How to Win Friends and Influence People* | Sincere compliments, remembering names, listening. |
| Vanessa Van Edwards, *Cues* | Body language and voice, with research behind it. |

Avoid "alpha/beta", "high-value man", anything that sneers at women, and
contested science sold as fact (power posing is one).
